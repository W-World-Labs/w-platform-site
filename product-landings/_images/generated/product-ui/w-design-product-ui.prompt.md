## Design Intent

Generate a product-card thumbnail that looks like a real W Platform application interface. The image should communicate the product's actual workflow through dashboards, tables, charts, records, pipelines, previews, or operational panels. Avoid generic glowing hubs, decorative nodes, unreadable AI-generated pseudo-logos, and sci-fi abstractions.
